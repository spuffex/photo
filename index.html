Paris — Montmartre

A historic car, and the streets of Montmartre that suit it.

A street photoshoot in Paris, built around one historic car and the old streets of Montmartre. The idea was to photograph the car like a portrait subject, letting the architecture and the light around it tell its story.

This is the frame that came out of it, together with the close details and the other angles that show how the shot was built.

Amsterdam — Model photography

Portfolios, built one shoot at a time.

Amsterdam is full of young aspiring models and photographers, all trying to build something of their own. I spent many sessions in the city's iconic places, creating portfolios for models and sharing photography knowledge with other photographers.

Each shoot was as much about collaboration as photography — testing ideas together, and leaving everyone with images they're proud of.

Indonesia — Ijen

A sulfur miner above the crater.

Travel photography in Indonesia. Among the landscapes, one scene stood out: a sulfur miner working over the volcano crater.

The main frame is a landscape that keeps the miner in the scale of the place, shown with the close details and wider angles that give it context.
