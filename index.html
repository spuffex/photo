Reel 014 — Ridge Line, October

The ridge waited for the light we almost missed.

Four hours up, in the dark, on the promise of a clear sky. The clouds didn't break until the last twenty minutes — and then, all at once, the whole valley turned to copper.

This is the frame that made the climb worth it, and the quieter moments either side of it that most people never see.

Reel 019 — Harbor Light, November

Low tide, and a town still waking up.

Fishing boats go out before anyone else is awake. I followed one crew for three mornings before the light, the tide, and the boat all lined up the way I'd pictured it.

What stayed with me wasn't the boat itself — it was the rope, the rust, the small worn details that only show up when you get close.

About

Fifteen years chasing the hour before dark.

Marco is a documentary and landscape photographer working primarily on medium-format film, based between the coast and the mountains. The work favors quiet, unhurried scenes — places and people caught in the light they'd choose for themselves.

Prints have been shown in group exhibitions across three countries, and clients include independent publishers, outdoor brands, and private collectors.
